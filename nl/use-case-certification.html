De uitdaging met certificeringsaudits

Most companies scramble before an audit. Documents are scattered, evidence is incomplete, procedures exist on paper but nobody follows them. The result? Failed audits, corrective actions, delays and lost contracts.

With Proceze, audit-readiness isn't a project — it's a state. Every procedure execution is logged, every training tracked, every version controlled. When the auditor arrives, you open the system and show them everything.
